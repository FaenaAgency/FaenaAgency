fæna is an independent firm working across brand strategy, creative direction, marketing and communications. We partner with ambitious businesses to define their point of view, shape their identity and turn their vision into meaningful work.

Built on strategic thinking, creative judgment and a respect for the craft behind every great brand.

  1. i.

    Talent, by name.

    Every designer, writer and maker is credited on the work they make. Always.

  2. ii.

    Craft, slowly.

    Few projects, full attention. Decisions made by hand, never by template.

  3. iii.

    Community, first.

    Clients become collaborators. Collaborators become the pack.
